## Complete analysis populations

The field atlas now has two populations: being descriptions and reported
abductions. Both expose the same 33 report-field comparisons. The abduction
population includes all 3,217 reported-abduction records, including the 1,644
without an indexed being description. Its source-classified and guarded
narrative rows partition the population; ambiguous mentions stay separately
identified and are never promoted to reported abductions.

Counts, within-row, within-column and supported-row percentages use explicit
distinct-report denominators. Multi-label rows and columns can overlap. Equal
source composition, source omission and supported source/decade/macroregion
conditional contrasts are descriptive sensitivity checks. Missing geography,
small cells, source overlap or weak common support remain visible. No p-values,
causal effects, biological species or independent-incident counts are claimed.

Source-field and narrative views use an additive origin sidecar traced from the
unchanged extractor on all 16,319 original indexed records. Normal and traced
classifier output must agree exactly. Every origin row joins the original event
ID, source, chunk, detail index and SHA-256. The source and narrative masks may
overlap; collection names and re-lexed excerpts are not used as provenance
proxies. Classifications and source profile totals remain available separately
from the selected comparison evidence mode.

## Report inspection

Cells and coverage buckets open bounded pages of original reports, including
unmapped reports. The worker uses the same field classifier and native context
membership decisions as the chart. Reading a cell changes neither map nor
filters. Opening the source record, locating a report and applying the exact
matching IDs are explicit actions. Applied IDs have a clearable Results banner.
Request identity, current filter signature, cancellation and scope checks prevent
stale pages from becoming current evidence.

The reviewed pilot contains 24 source entries across nine sources, 20 case
families, 25 episodes, 36 entity observations and 56 attributed fields. Of its
25 pinned report links, 22 are accepted and three remain candidate holds; one
source entry is unlinked. It attaches case families, episodes, recall stages, reported
interaction/onboard status, and entity-specific attributes to pinned reports.
Candidate and contradicted links remain labelled holds. A contact classification
alone does not establish abduction. Recurring histories are not expanded into
invented dated episodes. Conflicting source clocks, dates and identity evidence
remain visible; locality names and civil clocks do not become verified sites or
UTC timestamps. Only small factual metadata, source links and permitted short
excerpts enter the runtime. The pilot manifest and source review retain the exact
entry/link counts and reuse limits.
