## User-facing behavior

An initially collapsed **Encounters** panel under Categories adds separate
abduction and being filters. Named source labels and descriptions of appearance
are visibly grouped. Multiple selected being types match any selected type;
the abduction and being facets combine with the existing dates, categories,
keyword, geography and legend filters. **Include all report types** deliberately
widens the existing raw Type filter; choosing an encounter does not silently
change it. **Clear encounters** clears only the new facets, while the existing
full reset also resets these controls.

Result cards show neutral encounter badges. Full Details retains the original
source values/excerpts, extraction method, source collection, chunk/detail
locator, source hash and reported craft relationship. Source records and their
coordinates are not rewritten. Height and clothing are shown only where the
source supplies them; height alone does not invent a being category.

Analysis gains a native **Encounters** topic: abduction/being profiles, being ×
craft and abduction × craft counts, annual activity, source coverage and missing
classification. Exact tables and native exports accompany the charts. These
facets also restrict existing Analysis topics and comparisons through their
shared cohort. Unknown dates are retained in profiles but do not acquire
invented annual dates. Multiple being descriptions can make category totals
exceed the number of reports; a record counts at most once in each matrix cell.

## Evidence and interpretation

The complete preserved corpus is scanned into a small source-pinned sidecar.
The [primary CUFOS UFOCAT codebook](https://cufos.org/PDFs/UFOCAT%20Codebook%202023.pdf)
defines the `Ufonaut`, Height and Uniform fields and the relevant encounter
classifications. HYNEK CE4 supports source-classified abduction; VALLEE CE4 is
broader personal interaction/contact. TYPE 9A/9B combine abduction with other
possibilities, and TYPE 9/missing time alone do not establish abduction.

Explicit source classifications and guarded own-report narrative claims are
separate. Negation, speculation, dreams, another episode, a book/publication,
and conventional witnesses/aircraft can withhold narrative classification.
The original source wording remains inspectable. “No indexed evidence” is
missing classification, not an observation that no encounter occurred.

Reported beings do not automatically imply a craft. Relationship states are
explicit source-reported association, same-report co-reporting, no craft
reported, and not established. Only the first two enter craft–being count
matrices. TYPE 7/AN3/zero-object examples can be retained as being reports
while remaining outside those matrices. Counts describe catalog reports,
including repeated source accounts, rather than independent verified incidents
or biological species. The heatmaps use sequential counts and contain no
expected values, effect estimates, p-values or causal claims.

Delivery is atomic and hash-checked. The source-index payload validates catalog
size, schema, unique safe IDs, taxonomy, evidence fields and source locators.
An event/source identity mismatch cannot acquire badges or filter membership.
If the artifact is unavailable, encounter filters are disabled and normal map
exploration continues. Main and worker use the same codes and mask semantics.

## Reproduction and storage lifecycle

`scripts/build_encounter_evidence.py` streams and hashes the existing 282 detail
chunks. The executed pre-review extractor is retained at
`validated/held_extractor_before_review_holds.py` because the final builder adds
the reviewed sparse refinement. `validated/reviewed/audit.json` pins that base,
the source inputs, extractor, quality review and replay command. Replaying with
`--apply-reviewed-holds` and a fresh output directory verifies the two original
source records before withdrawing their narrative-only positive classifications.
It does not alter the canonical reports or any direct source-coded facts.
Existing artifact paths are protected against overwrite.

The initial candidate at the research root and the guarded base at `validated/`
are retained as unique quality intermediates. They document the false-positive
corrections, preserve executed extractors and permit replay. Their small review
receipts are scientific provenance, not additional deployment rollback copies.
The final `quality_transition_receipt.json` verifies that all 46,010 direct
source-coded evidence entries survive unchanged and that the 19 reviewed alias
traps are withheld. Earlier runtime receipts are explicitly named
`corpus-runtime-held.json` and `corpus-runtime-before-reviewed-holds.json`.
All current results use the final reviewed artifact.
