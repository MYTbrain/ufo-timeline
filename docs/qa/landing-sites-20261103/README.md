# Landing site diagrams and combined analysis

The Landing Sites workspace separates three evidence products.

- Case details and diagrams preserve each reviewed observation, source locator and geometry limitation. Diagram layers describe ground impressions, scorching, vegetation deformation and other surface traces separately.
- Combined analysis counts reviewed dossiers once per trace and layout cell. Several layouts can occur at one site. Swirl and twist are separated from other deformation, and uncertain handedness is retained. A shared plan is counted only when its two trace kinds can be toggled separately. Length spreads and span ratios use the recorded measurements with their original roles.
- Catalog inventories preserve source records, candidate keywords, classification codes and possible overlaps. These records support discovery and source review. Their counts are kept separate from site geometry and are not estimates of independent incidents or frequencies.

## Phillips screening

The case-page screening covers all 111 numbered PDF pages 19–129 of Phillips’s 1975 CUFOS edition. The early receipt records review coverage, promotions and held candidates. The later pages 72–129 were screened with OCR keywords and checked visually for embedded plans. Pages 126–129 list abbreviated entries with insufficient detail. Front-matter illustration pages 6–18 were checked separately for site plans and photographs. Pages 10, 12 and 15 support a separate Chapeau ring 2 diagram and the revised three-layer Houston diagram. The Delroy schematic uses the stated concentric dimensions on case page 54. The Chapeau photograph and catalog give no common plan for all four reported formations. The Hampton Lakes, Tucson and Columbia photographs give no shared trace geometry. The combined view shows Phillips’s original summary percentages from PDF page 135 in a separate table. The printed charts give no denominators or joint counts on that page.

Supplemental visual checks on pages 75, 97, 111, 115 and 122 support Brasília, La Criolla, Lucerne Valley, St. Matthias, Lemon Grove and Langenburg. The pass screens the numbered catalog pages for geometry candidates, then checks the selected source records. Scan recognition, ambiguous case numbers, short abstracts and unavailable upstream field plans leave residual coverage limits. Several reported arrangements can be shown only as labelled schematics. The review does not estimate the incidence of a shape among all landings or test a heat or RF mechanism.
