# Phillips catalog diagram screen, PDF pages 19–71

Source is the July 1975 CUFOS edition of Ted Phillips’s *Physical Traces Associated with UFO Sightings*. The PDF page number includes the cover and front matter. Printed catalog page equals PDF page minus 18. The retained source SHA-256 is `e824270528264c2563df17f79540171dc216fb9be85968a5eb844ecb1b2be17b`. [Publisher scan](https://cufos.org/PDFs/books/Physical_Traces.pdf#page=19)

The screen covered all 53 PDF pages from 19 through 71, corresponding to printed pages 1 through 53. The starting inventory linked 284 case numbers to those pages. The screening pass used fresh page-by-page OCR and read around hits for measurements, shape, count, spacing, impressions, scorching, displacement, and handedness. The printed scan was visually sampled on 18 candidate pages (28, 32, 37, 46–48, 50, 52, 57–61, 63, 65, 67, 69 and 70). Front-matter pages 6–9, 14 and 16–18 were separately checked for plans or photos connected to this range. The page screen includes continuations and can surface cases missed by the numbered OCR inventory. It does not establish a visual review of every abstract or rule out other diagram candidates.

A diagram needed a site plan or sufficiently specific trace dimensions, positions or spatial relations. Prose can support a labelled schematic. A shape attached to the reported object, a mark count alone, or a single width without a shape does not establish a site layout. The drawings omit marks whose locations are unknown.
