## Scientific preservation

The atlas is an optimization of the pinned original Astronomy Engine calculation. It preserves the original adaptive sample subset over the 50-hour civil-date UTC interval, apparent geocentric frame, date-role gates, Gregorian exclusions, category-specific year-month opportunity, sampled stable/possible bounds, and explicitly unavailable local houses. Longitudes and precession remain Float64. Rates use Float32 with an exact original-ephemeris fallback near classification thresholds. Verified arbitrary UTC timestamps and unsupported atlas dates also fall back to the original calculation; those calls are explicitly counted in exported evidence.

Binary dimensions, epoch, schema, bodies, time grid, numeric formats, finite values, engine identity and gzip/decompressed hashes are checked before installation. A malformed replacement cannot overwrite previously validated source evidence or atlas state. `PLANETARY_PRECOMPUTATION_METHOD_REVIEW_20261007.md` documents the independent method review.
