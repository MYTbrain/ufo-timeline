# Independent recovery of unmapped original accounts

The final batch accepts **148 original-account locality references** and **one date-precision downgrade**, affecting **149 distinct source records**. These are sparse reviewed edits, not changes to the canonical corpus or a new production release. The 148 locations comprise 140 MUFON and eight NUFORC originals: 133 United States and 15 Canada references.

This lane screened all **3,440** original-unmapped / UPDB-mapped reverse links that pass the strict single-member, native-identity, stored-day, same-craft and substantial narrative-agreement gates. The linked UPDB point is deliberately absent from the geographic-selection inputs. A mapped republication does not establish that its point is correct, and a recovered original is not a newly independent physical incident.

## What qualifies for a point

The original native observation place must explicitly name the locality and jurisdiction. Exact primary names, registered geographic aliases, punctuation/spacing normalization, repeated matching state text, and bounded geographic abbreviations are supported; unsupported spelling correction is withheld. Each accepted original was read from its pinned shared detail chunk, checked against the eight before-value guards and single-member provenance, and required an exact original observed-day field. Submission dates are never borrowed.

Geographic selection uses the independently pinned shared GeoNames archive, including exact alternate names within the declared country and state/province. Every matching populated-place reference participates in the homonym screen, including historical-name competitors. A cluster spanning more than 10 km is withheld. Administrative feature coordinates are not eligible, and selected-point coincidences with administrative markers are screened separately. These are named locality references, **not measured object locations or exact observer coordinates**.

The 148 references comprise 108 repeated matching jurisdiction-text recoveries, 24 exact name/registered-alias recoveries, ten bounded geographic-abbreviation recoveries and six city-sector municipality references. The latter preserve the original directional wording, use `location_precision=approximate`, and explicitly state that the point represents the municipality rather than the sector or observer. Independent municipal authorities support the accepted SE Portland, northwest Leavenworth, Northwest Houston, North Scottsdale and North Phoenix area names. The authority registry restricts which source forms qualify; it does not strip North/South from unrelated municipality names such as North Huntingdon or South Brunswick.

## Source research changed the decisions

Nine official NUFORC original-page retrievals were attempted. Six were accessible and three were unavailable or returned 403. Those live checks supplement record-specific pinned original evidence; they do not authorize blanket extrapolation to other accounts, and an inaccessible page is not treated as a missing report. Official city sources independently support the municipal references and Danbury/El Centro parser cases.

The [native NUFORC 125959 report](https://nuforc.org/sighting/?id=125959) describes ISS live-feed screenshots. Its Joppatown submission locality does not establish a local sighting point, so it remains unmapped. MUFON 34927 describes camping at Kananaskis Lakes west of Calgary; the submitted Calgary field is a regional locator, and its city point is withheld. MUFON 50015 places the observer on the Chehalis River while distinguishing a Maple Ridge home from the Chilliwack submitted field; this needs separate observer-site research. MUFON 54503 describes hiking near a specific trailhead, so its distance/site reference is retained for research instead of replacing it with a municipality center.

MUFON **66391**, event **4389926031492767**, explicitly describes its date as “Sometime around the 15 th of Nov 1967.” The accepted date-only decision changes `exact_day` to `approximate`. The sorting anchor remains **1967-11-15**, and all date fields, raw narrative and source provenance remain unchanged. Its coordinate recovery is withheld. Full original evidence is retained with its native ID, raw occurrence field and exact detail locator.

## Unresolved work remains explicit

There are **3,292 links without an accepted coordinate decision**. First-failure categories retain: 733 route/compound/unsupported source-place strings; 737 unsupported or conflicting source-jurisdiction structures; 130 previously accepted originals; 43 parallel-owned accounts; 1,519 names lacking an exact registered geographic match in the declared jurisdiction; 28 city-sector cases lacking supported municipality authority; 46 distant same-jurisdiction homonym clusters; 47 observer-route/mobile contexts; two separately named observer sites; one remote-imagery observation; five uncertain observed-day cases; and one explicit submitted-city/observer-site disagreement. The one accepted precision downgrade remains in the coordinate-unresolved queue by design.

The queue preserves each original source name, native account key and reason. Typos, named sites, conflicting state fields, ambiguous city areas and uncertain dates remain researchable. This pass does not claim those cases are absent, false, duplicates to be deleted, or safely geocodable from existing republication points.

## Verification and retention

All **149 accepted decisions** were applied read-only against their actual pinned original chunk rows through the shared quality reader. Coordinate records passed original native/member/provenance guards; the date-only record additionally passed the native ID and single-member checks. Sorting dates, location text, raw fields, narratives and source provenance were preserved; base rows were not mutated. Eight invalid-edit controls were rejected, and nine source-role/geographic-ambiguity controls passed. The final overlap screen against 3,581 other accepted IDs was empty before handoff.

The full-source receipt covers **204 researched originals in 150 pinned source chunks**, including rejected candidates, with original raw fields, source rows, complete narratives, guard values, precision and detail locators. The initial context-review file preserves unique first-pass evidence, including candidates subsequently blocked or rejected; it is a retained research trail, not another catalog or rollback copy.

Canonical lane artifacts are the two sealed accepted sidecars plus the evidence, authority registry, census and validation receipts. The shared unmodified canonical base and retained quality package/release remain the rollback; no new deployment or rollback release was created here. No full corpus/archive was copied, no staging tree was created, no protected analysis was deleted, and no file exceeds 100 MiB. `storage_inventory.json` gives exact sizes and hashes. No cleanup of unique research evidence is proposed.

Rebuild with `research_reverse_recovery.py`, then `finalize_reverse_recovery.py`, then `verify_geographic_controls.py`, against the pinned shared inputs and retained authority/manual-review records.
